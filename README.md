# Myntra Product Management MVP

This repository contains two separate deliverables for the Myntra PM Fellowship final project:

## 1. AI Discovery Engine
Located in `discovery-engine/`

The AI Discovery Engine analyzes app reviews and videos to find product opportunities.

## 2. Wishlist Decision Copilot MVP
Located in `wishlist-decision-copilot/`

The Wishlist Decision Copilot is a standalone MVP built to increase the purchase conversion rate of wishlisted items by helping users resolve information uncertainty directly within the app.
